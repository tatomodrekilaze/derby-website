## Free publishing sequence

- First post: the entrance and a readable English location caption. Pin it so visitors can find the door.
- Second post: a real pool shot with the day/evening rate distinction on screen.
- Third post: a real snooker shot and the 20 GEL hourly rate.
- Keep one English highlight covering prices, address, opening hours and phone reservations.
- Reply to incoming questions in the visitor's language when possible. Never promise a table before availability has been confirmed by the club.
- Offer the English visitor link to hotel/hostel staff and tour guides who are willing to recommend local activities. Respect group rules when sharing in travel communities; do not bulk-message people or post repetitive links.
