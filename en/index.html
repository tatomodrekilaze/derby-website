Visiting Tbilisi?

Play billiards in Gldani, Tbilisi

Looking for somewhere to play pool or snooker in Tbilisi? Derby offers pool, snooker and Russian billiards in Gldani. Here is what you need to plan a game during your visit.

Where is Derby and how do I get there?

Find us at 3 Marat Nozadze Street, Gldani, Tbilisi. Open our Google Maps location for a route from your hotel or wherever you are staying. Look for the red Derby neon sign at the entrance.

How much does a table cost?

Pool costs 10 GEL per table per hour from 12:00 to 18:00, then 15 GEL from 18:00 until closing. Snooker is 20 GEL per hour and Russian billiards is 18 GEL per hour. Prices are per table, not per person, and are shown in Georgian lari (GEL).

How late is the club open?

Derby is open every day from 12:00 midday until 03:00 the following morning. All times are local Tbilisi time.

How do I reserve a table?

Call +995 568 03 05 05 with your preferred game, date and time. The +995 is Georgia's country code. Reservations and table availability are confirmed by phone.
